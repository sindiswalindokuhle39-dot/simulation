Riet River Health Lab

Grade 11 Life Sciences · Pollution and freshwater ecosystem health · about 40 minutes

Mystery: Learners at Mokala Secondary found dead fish in the Riet River at sunrise, yet the water looked fine at midday. Why? Use the model to investigate how pollution, heat, low flow and the time of day change dissolved oxygen, then decide how the community can fix it. This is a simplified teaching model, not real Riet data.

Outcomes: predict and test how nutrients, sewage, litter, flow and temperature affect oxygen and invertebrates; read a 24-hour graph; explain eutrophication; judge a feasible, affordable solution.

Step 1: Predict

Extra nutrients (nitrates and phosphates) from fertiliser cause algae to grow. Algae photosynthesise in daylight, but respire all the time, and when they die, decomposers use oxygen.
